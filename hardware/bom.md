# Bill of Materials (BOM)

Compact Tasmota web radio streamer — v1 (mini, line / headphone out).

| # | Component | Details | Notes |
|---|-----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | ESP32-S3 Super Mini | ESP32-S3 with PSRAM, USB-C, onboard WS2812 LED | Main controller, native USB |
| 2 | PCM5102A DAC module | I2S audio DAC with 3.5 mm line / headphone jack | Purple breakout board |
| 3 | USB-C cable | Power + flashing | 5 V supply |
| 4 | 3D-printed enclosure | 33 × 36 × 15 mm, 2 parts | Onshape model linked in README |
| 5 | Countersunk screw | Front plate fixing | 1 pc |
| 6 | Hook-up wire | Power (5 V / GND) and I2S signals | Thin gauge |

The two boards are stacked. A plastic connector body (without its metal pins) is
reused as an insulating spacer between them.
