# Bill of Materials (BOM)

Compact Tasmota web radio streamer — v1 (mini, line / headphone out).

| # | Component | Details | Where to buy |
|---|-----------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | ESP32-S3 Super Mini | ESP32-S3 with PSRAM, USB-C, onboard WS2812 LED | [AliExpress](https://a.aliexpress.com/_c3mvegP5) |
| 2 | PCM5102A DAC module | I2S audio DAC with 3.5 mm line / headphone jack | [AliExpress](https://a.aliexpress.com/_c3kTVVpz) |
| 3 | USB-C cable | Power + flashing (5 V) | any |
| 4 | 3D-printed enclosure | 33 × 36 × 15 mm, 2 parts | files in [`3d/`](../3d/) |
| 5 | Countersunk screw | Front plate fixing (1 pc) | any |
| 6 | Hook-up wire | Power (5 V / GND) and I2S signals, thin gauge | any |

The two boards are stacked. A plastic connector body (without its metal pins) is
reused as an insulating spacer between them.

> Store links point to the exact modules used in this build. Product listings on
> AliExpress can change or expire over time; if a link is dead, search for
> "ESP32-S3 Super Mini" and "PCM5102A DAC module".
